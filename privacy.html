PRIVACY POLICY

Your choices stay optional.

Effective: August 14, 2026. Last updated: August 27, 2026. This policy describes the current HSGame iOS release.

Overview

HSGame is an offline-first arcade game. It does not require an account and does not sell personal information. Game progress, settings, mission state, and earned rewards are stored locally on the device.

Optional rewarded advertising

HSGame may offer optional Google AdMob rewarded ads. Core play does not require an ad. Google Mobile Ads and its consent platform may process an IP address, coarse location derived from IP, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, and crash, performance, or other diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, consent, and service operation.

Personalized advertising or cross-app tracking is used only where the player has provided the required consent and App Tracking Transparency permission. HSGame configures ads for teen-or-older content and disables Google’s persistent publisher first-party identifier.

Consent and choices

Where required, HSGame displays Google’s consent form before requesting ads. Ad Privacy Choices appears in the app’s Settings whenever Google requires a persistent privacy-options entry point. Tracking permission can be changed in the iOS Settings app. Refusing consent or tracking does not block any game.

Notifications

Mission and Rover reminders are optional local notifications. Notification scheduling is performed on the device after permission is granted. HSGame does not operate a remote notification server.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until the app is deleted or its storage is cleared. Consent records and advertising data are retained according to Google’s policies and applicable law. You can withdraw available advertising consent through HSGame Settings and can contact support about privacy requests.

Children

HSGame is not directed to children and is not submitted to the App Store Kids category.

Third-party information

Advertising services are provided by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.
