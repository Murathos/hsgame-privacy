PRIVACY POLICY

Your choices stay optional.

Effective: August 14, 2026. Last updated: August 28, 2026. This policy describes the current HSGame iOS release.

Overview

HSGame is an offline-first arcade game. It does not require an account and does not sell personal information. Game progress, settings, mission state, and earned rewards are stored locally on the device.

V1 advertising status

Rewarded advertising is disabled in HSGame V1. This release does not initialize Google Mobile Ads or its consent platform, request advertising consent or App Tracking Transparency for ads, load or present ads, or grant ad-based extra rewards. Core play is fully usable offline.

The optional AdMob, consent, rewarded-ad, and campaign-measurement integrations remain prepared in the product for a later update. When advertising is enabled in a future release, Google and Meta may process information described in that release’s privacy notice and App Store disclosures.

Consent and choices

HSGame V1 does not request advertising consent or App Tracking Transparency because advertising is disabled in this release. The games remain fully playable without network access or advertising. A future ad-enabled release will display Google’s consent form and provide Ad Privacy Choices where required.

Notifications

Mission and Rover reminders are optional local notifications. Notification scheduling is performed on the device after permission is granted. HSGame does not operate a remote notification server.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until the app is deleted or its storage is cleared. V1 does not retain advertising consent or advertising data because advertising is disabled. You can contact support about privacy requests.

Children

HSGame is not directed to children and is not submitted to the App Store Kids category.

Third-party information

No advertising service is active in V1. The prepared future integrations are provided by Google and Meta. See Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information for advertising, and Meta’s Privacy Policy for the later ad-enabled release.
